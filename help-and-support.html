Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions asked by citizens using the Infrastructure Damage Reporting System.

How do I report infrastructure damage?
Navigate to the Report Damage page, upload a clear image, choose the correct damage category, enter the location, provide a detailed description, and submit your complaint.
How do I track my complaint?
Open the Track Complaint page and enter your Complaint ID to view its latest progress and current status.
Can I upload multiple images?
Yes. Uploading multiple clear photographs helps the authorities verify the reported issue more efficiently.
Can I edit my complaint after submission?
Once submitted, complaints cannot be edited. If you need to provide additional information, please contact support through the Feedback & Suggestions page.
What image formats are accepted?
JPG, JPEG and PNG images are supported. Make sure the uploaded photos are clear and not blurry.
How long does complaint verification take?
Verification time depends on the severity and location of the issue. Most reports are reviewed within a few working days.
Will I receive updates?
Yes. Complaint status changes such as Under Review, Assigned, In Progress and Resolved can be viewed using the Track Complaint page.
Can I report more than one issue?
Yes. Every infrastructure issue should be submitted as a separate complaint for faster processing.

Complaint Status Guide

Understand what each complaint status means during the resolution process.

Submitted

Your complaint has been successfully received by the system.

Under Review

Authorities are reviewing the complaint details and verifying the issue.

Assigned

The complaint has been assigned to the responsible department.

In Progress

Repair work has started and is currently underway.

Resolved

The reported infrastructure issue has been successfully repaired.

Tips for Better Reporting

These simple tips help authorities verify and resolve complaints faster.

Upload Clear Photos

Take high-quality pictures in daylight whenever possible.

Provide Accurate Location

Mention nearby landmarks or use GPS for precise identification.

Write a Detailed Description

Explain the issue clearly to help authorities understand the problem.

Avoid Duplicate Reports

Search before reporting to ensure the issue has not already been submitted.
