Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about reporting infrastructure damage.

How do I report infrastructure damage?

Click on the "Report Now" button, fill in the details, upload an image and submit your complaint.

Can I track my complaint?

Yes. Every complaint receives a unique Report ID that can be used to track its current status.

Can I upload multiple images?

Yes. Citizens can upload multiple images to help authorities verify the issue quickly.

Is my personal information secure?

Absolutely. Your account information is protected and only authorized officials can access complaint details.
