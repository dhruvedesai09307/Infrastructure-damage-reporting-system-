Why Community Awareness?

Every citizen plays an important role in maintaining roads, public buildings, parks, water systems, and streetlights. Together we can reduce accidents, improve infrastructure, and build a smarter city.

Road Safety Guidelines

Following simple road safety practices helps prevent accidents and protects everyone.

Wear Safety Gear

Always wear helmets while riding two-wheelers and use seat belts in cars.

Obey Traffic Signals

Follow traffic lights, speed limits and road signs at all times.

Avoid Phone Usage

Never use mobile phones while driving to reduce distractions.

Use Zebra Crossing

Pedestrians should always cross roads using designated crossings.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about community awareness and citizen participation.

Why is community awareness important?

Community awareness encourages citizens to work together to protect public infrastructure, reduce accidents, and create a cleaner environment.

How can I participate?

You can report infrastructure damage, volunteer for campaigns, attend awareness events, and encourage others to use the IDRS platform.

Is reporting damage free?

Yes. Reporting infrastructure damage through the IDRS platform is completely free for all citizens.

Can I become a volunteer?

Yes. Anyone interested in helping the community can join awareness campaigns and volunteer programs.

How can I report infrastructure damage?

Citizens can report infrastructure damage through the IDRS platform by uploading a photo, selecting the location, and submitting the report.
