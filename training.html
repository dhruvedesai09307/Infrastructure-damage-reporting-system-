Core Training Modules

Explore essential procedures for identifying, documenting, and classifying public infrastructure hazards.

Module 01
Hazard Categorization
Learn how to accurately identify damage categories including roadway potholes, water pipeline bursts, broken streetlights, and traffic signal outages.
  • Distinguish structural vs electrical hazards
  • Select correct report sub-categories
  • Prevent misclassified ticket delays
Module 02
Evidence Capture & Photo Standards
High-quality visual evidence accelerates verification. Capture wide-angle context shots along with close-up defect details safely.
  • Maintain minimum 3m safety distance
  • Capture nearby landmark for context
  • Avoid blurry or night-glare photos
Module 03
Geo-Tagging & Address Precision
Municipal repair crews rely on precise coordinates. Master pinpointing GPS locations and detailing exact street landmarks.
  • Enable high-accuracy device GPS
  • Include nearest house/pole number
  • Specify lane direction & intersection
Module 04
Severity Assessment & Emergency Protocol
Understand severity levels (Low, Medium, High, Critical Emergency) and learn when to contact emergency dispatchers immediately.
  • Identify active live-wire risks
  • Recognize major structural collapse
  • Emergency hotline contact guidelines

Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

4-Step workflow to follow every time you submit a damage report.

1

Assess Safety First

Never step into active traffic or touch exposed wires. Stand in a safe pedestrian area before documenting.

2

Capture Photo Evidence

Take 1-2 clear photographs showing the damage and visible surrounding context landmarks.

3

Pinpoint Location

Use auto-location or tap the map to place the marker exactly where the hazard exists.

4

Submit & Track

Submit your report to receive a tracking ID. Monitor live status updates as dispatch teams respond.

Reporting Best Practices

Ensure your contributions meet municipal compliance standards.

Recommended Do's
  • Do provide concise, factual descriptions of the hazard.
  • Do check if the issue has already been reported on the Live Map.
  • Do ensure photos are well-lit and clearly show the damage extent.
  • Do report immediately upon discovery to prevent public accidents.
Strict Don'ts
  • Don't submit fake, exaggerated, or test reports on public portal.
  • Don't trespass on private property to take photographs.
  • Don't include personal faces or vehicle license plates in photos.
  • Don't attempt to repair municipal electrical or water lines yourself.
