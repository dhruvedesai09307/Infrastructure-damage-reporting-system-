About Project

Infrastructure Damage Reporting System

A smart digital platform designed to make reporting, tracking and managing public infrastructure problems simple, transparent and efficient. Citizens can report damaged infrastructure and authorities can manage, prioritize and resolve reported issues through one centralized system.

Project Overview

Connecting citizens and authorities for better public infrastructure

What is this project?

The Infrastructure Damage Reporting System is a web-based platform that allows citizens to report problems related to public infrastructure such as damaged roads, potholes, broken street lights, drainage problems, damaged public property and other infrastructure issues.


The system helps authorities receive accurate information, review submitted reports, assign issues to responsible departments and update their progress until the problem is resolved.

Project Objective

  • Easy infrastructure issue reporting
  • Faster response from authorities
  • Transparent report tracking
  • Centralized complaint management
  • Better public infrastructure planning

Key Features

Important features provided by the system

Online Damage Reporting

Citizens can submit infrastructure damage reports through an easy-to-use online form.

Photo Evidence

Users can attach images to provide visual evidence of the reported infrastructure problem.

Location Information

Report location details help authorities identify the exact area where an issue exists.

Report Tracking

Citizens can track submitted reports and check the current status of their complaints.

Status Updates

Users can receive updates when their complaint is reviewed, assigned or resolved.

Analytics Dashboard

Authorities can analyze reports and monitor infrastructure problems using useful statistics.

Issue Categories

Problems can be organized into categories such as roads, lighting, drainage and public property.

Community Participation

Citizens can actively participate in improving public infrastructure by reporting issues, sharing valuable feedback, supporting local initiatives, and working together with authorities to build safer, cleaner, and smarter communities.
