Using the Platform

Infrastructure Care provides a platform through which citizens can report visible damage or problems affecting public infrastructure.

The platform may be used to report issues such as:

  • Damaged roads and potholes.
  • Broken street lights.
  • Water leaks and drainage problems.
  • Traffic signal failures.
  • Damaged footpaths.
  • Fallen trees affecting public roads.
  • Damaged bridges.
  • Problems with public facilities.

The platform is intended for community improvement and responsible reporting.

04

Reporting Standards

Reports should be submitted honestly, accurately and in good faith. Providing clear information helps authorities understand and investigate reported problems.

A useful report should contain:

  • A clear description of the issue.
  • An accurate location.
  • Relevant photographs when available.
  • Additional information that may help identify the problem.
  • Correct contact information when required.

Users should avoid submitting the same issue repeatedly unless the situation has changed significantly.

Safety comes first Never enter a dangerous area or stand in traffic to take photographs. Your safety is more important than submitting a report.
