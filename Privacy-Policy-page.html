Information We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to process damage reports and improve public infrastructure.

  • Contact details – name, email, phone number
  • Report data – location, state, city, photos, description
  • Device info – browser type, IP address (anonymised)
  • Usage data – pages visited, timestamp
Minimised collection

How We Use Data

Your data helps us verify, route, and resolve infrastructure issues efficiently.

  • Route reports to responsible municipal authorities
  • Analyse trends for preventive maintenance
  • Communicate status updates on your report
  • Improve system performance & user experience
No automated decisions

Data Sharing

We share data only with relevant public agencies and service partners.

  • Municipal corporations & public works departments
  • Utility services (water, electricity, road safety)
  • Emergency services (if immediate hazard detected)
  • Secure hosting & encrypted analytics infrastructure
Strict confidentiality

Data Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information.

  • Encrypted transmission (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Access controls & regular security audits
  • Anonymised analytics logging
  • Data backup & disaster recovery protocols
