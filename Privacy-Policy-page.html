Information We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to process damage reports and improve public infrastructure.

  • Contact details – email, phone (optional)
  • Report data – location, description, photos
  • Device info – browser type, IP (anonymised)
  • Usage data – pages visited, time spent
minimised collection

How We Use Data

Your data helps us verify, route, and resolve infrastructure issues efficiently.

  • Route reports to responsible authorities
  • Analyse trends for preventive maintenance
  • Communicate updates on your report
  • Improve system performance & user experience
no automated decisions

Data Sharing

We share data only with relevant public agencies and service partners.

  • Municipal corporations & road departments
  • Utility companies (water, electricity)
  • Emergency services (if immediate danger)
  • Third-party hosting & analytics (anonymised)
strict confidentiality

Data Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information.

  • Encrypted transmission (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Access controls & regular audits
  • Anonymised analytics
  • Data backup & disaster recovery
ISO 27001 aligned
