Safety Categories

Select a category to learn important safety precautions.

Road Safety

Potholes & damaged roads

Stay alert around potholes, cracks and damaged road surfaces.

  • Keep a safe distance from damaged areas.
  • Slow down when approaching potholes.
  • Never take photos while driving.
  • Report the location accurately.

Street Light Safety

Broken & non-functional lights

Damaged street lights and poles can create safety hazards.

  • Stay away from damaged poles.
  • Never touch exposed electrical wiring.
  • Report non-functional lights.
  • Mention the nearest landmark.

Water Leakage

Pipeline leaks & water wastage

Water leakage can cause slippery surfaces and road damage.

  • Avoid walking through unknown water.
  • Stay away from damaged pipelines.
  • Keep electrical equipment away from water.
  • Report the exact leakage location.

Traffic Signal Safety

Damaged or inactive signals

Broken traffic signals can create dangerous road conditions.

  • Follow traffic signs when signals fail.
  • Drive slowly near the affected junction.
  • Do not stand in moving traffic.
  • Report the junction location.

Fallen Tree Safety

Road & pathway obstruction

Fallen trees may block roads, pathways or damage nearby utilities.

  • Keep away from unstable branches.
  • Do not attempt to move the tree.
  • Watch for nearby electrical wires.
  • Report the obstruction promptly.

Bridge Safety

Cracks & structural damage

Structural damage can make bridges and elevated areas unsafe.

  • Do not enter restricted areas.
  • Stay away from visible structural damage.
  • Follow warning signs and barriers.
  • Report serious damage immediately.

Public Building Safety

Government building damage

Damaged public buildings may contain unsafe structural areas.

  • Avoid entering visibly unsafe structures.
  • Follow official warning signs.
  • Keep away from damaged sections.
  • Report the building location.

Sewer & Drainage Safety

Open drains & sewer issues

Open drains and missing covers can be dangerous for pedestrians.

  • Stay away from uncovered drains.
  • Never enter a sewer or drainage area.
  • Keep children away from open areas.
  • Report missing covers immediately.

Important Safety Notice

Never put yourself in danger to collect information. If an area appears unsafe, maintain a safe distance and report the issue with the information you can safely provide.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about safe reporting.

Can I report damage without taking a photo?
Yes. If taking a photo is unsafe, do not take one. You can provide the location and a clear description of the issue instead.
Should I enter a damaged area to inspect it?
No. Stay outside restricted or unsafe areas. Your personal safety should always come first.
What should I do if the damage is an emergency?
If the damage poses an immediate danger to people, contact the appropriate emergency services first. Then, submit a report through the system when it is safe.
